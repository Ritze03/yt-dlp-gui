# Changelog

All notable user-facing changes, newest first. Categories:

**Added** (new features),
**Fixed** (bug and behaviour fixes),
**Removed** (things taken out),
**Info** (notes worth knowing).

## 2026-10-26

### Added
- **Paste from clipboard**: Ctrl+V pastes a link into the input.
- **App icon**: the icon now shows in the window header.

## 2026-10-24

### Added
- **Easier install**: the app can now be installed with ritz-get.

## 2026-10-13

### Added
- **Cancel all**: one button cancels every download, another clears the cancelled ones.

### Fixed
- **Clear completed button**: it is no longer cut off.

### Info
- **New name**: the app is now called yt-dlp-gui.

## 2026-10-12

### Added
- **First release**: download videos and playlists in a simple window, with format
  choices, a download queue and light and dark themes.
- **Playlists**: tick the videos you want from a playlist.
- **Subtitles**: automatic captions are used when a video has no subtitles.
- **Browser logins**: use your browser's login for age-restricted videos.
- **Remembered format**: the app remembers your last format choice.

### Fixed
- **Playlist window**: its buttons are no longer cut off or unclickable.
- **Leftover files**: removing a download also removes its leftover files.
- **Retry**: a retried download uses your current settings.
